# <short name: the decision, not the feature>

**One-line decision.** At the single moment this scenario is about, what is the
teammate choosing between? If this needs "and then", split the scenario.

## Situation

Concrete and specific. What is being worked on, how far in, and what has just been
hit. Real enough to disagree with -- a situation nobody could picture is one nobody
can check the guidance against.

## What a good teammate does

One move. Not a sequence.

## What a bad teammate does

Plausible failures only, and ideally more than one kind. A strawman teaches nothing
because no guidance was ever needed to avoid it. The useful bad behaviours are the
ones a reasonable agent would actually produce.

## How you would tell, from outside

Written as something observable, because this becomes the rubric. Not "it used good
judgment" -- what would you SEE that distinguishes the good run from the bad one?

## What the good one knows

The knowledge, instinct, or rule that produced the right move. This becomes the
guidance, so it should be transferable rather than specific to this situation.

## Its twin

Where the same signal is present and the right move is the opposite. Name it, or
say explicitly why this scenario has no twin.

## Open questions

What we have not settled. Better recorded than resolved prematurely.
